ISS42 MOTOR AND DRIVER — TECHNICAL REFERENCE
==========================================

1. OVERVIEW AND SOURCE NOTES
----------------------------
Two-phase stepper motor with an integrated driver and closed-loop
position control.

This document consolidates the supplied ISS42 information and the settings
from an image titled "Nema 17 Closed Loop Stepper Driver Characteristics".
The image does not identify an exact brand or model. Its settings are
presented below as supplied; their applicability to the ISS42 is not
independently confirmed. NEMA 17 refers to the motor mounting size.

4. CONTROL SIGNALS AND ALARM OUTPUT
-----------------------------------
STEP — PU+ / PU-
- Rising-edge triggered: each low-to-high transition commands one microstep.
- Pulse width must be greater than 2.5 µs.

DIR — DR+ / DR-
- Direction is selected by a high or low logic level.
- Set DIR at least 50 µs before the STEP pulse.

ENABLE — EN+ / EN-
- Connecting EN+ to +5 V and holding EN- low cuts off the phase current.
- While disabled, the motor is free to turn, has no holding torque, and
  the driver does not respond to STEP pulses.
- Leave EN terminals unconnected if this function is not needed.
- EN can also clear the excessive position error alarm.

ALARM — AM+ / AM-
- Activated by overvoltage, undervoltage, phase loss, or excessive
  position error.
- Maximum output current: 50 mA.
- Default state at power-up: normally open (NO).

forward rotation, high-current mode, and 3200 pulses/revolution.


6. MOTION CALCULATIONS
----------------------
For N command pulses per revolution:
- Angle per pulse (degrees) = 360 / N.
- Pulses for a given angle = angle (degrees) x N / 360.
- Pulse frequency (Hz) = RPM x N / 60.
- RPM = pulse frequency (Hz) x 60 / N.
- Motor full steps/revolution = 360 / full-step angle (degrees).
- Microstep factor = N / motor full steps per revolution.

Example: at 3200 pulses/revolution with a 1.8° motor, the microstep
factor is 16. Each pulse commands 0.1125°; a 90° move requires 800 pulses,
and 60 RPM requires a pulse frequency of 3200 Hz.

8. ENVIRONMENT AND INSTALLATION
-------------------------------
Cooling:                Natural convection; fan-assisted cooling permitted
Operating temperature:  0 °C to 50 °C
Operating humidity:     40% to 90% RH
Vibration:              10–55 Hz; amplitude 0.15 mm
Storage temperature:    -20 °C to 65 °C

Do not install next to heat-generating equipment or in areas with dust
(including conductive dust), oil mist, corrosive or combustible gases,
excessive humidity, or strong vibration.
